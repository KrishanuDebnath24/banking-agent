Demo Bank

Privacy notice

Demo Bank assistant is a demonstration of an AI banking assistant. It is not a bank and never holds real money. This notice explains, in plain words, what it keeps about you, why, and for how long.

What it keeps, and why

  • Your email address and first name, from Google or from the address you type: to sign you in, to send you sign-in and security codes, and to keep your demo bank yours.
  • Your pretend bank: made-up accounts and transactions, and anything you change in it (transfers, an address you type, requests).
  • Your conversations with the assistant and the steps it took, so you can see and continue them.
  • A scrambled, one-way version of your internet address, only to limit sign-ups and sign-in codes per network. Your actual internet address is not stored.
  • Sign-in and security codes, stored scrambled so they can't be read.
  • Usage and cost records (model calls, time taken, cost), without your messages or email address.

How long

  • Your demo bank, conversations, requests and sign-in session: deleted 24 hours after your bank was opened, or straight away if you press "Start over".
  • Sign-in and security code records: deleted after a day.
  • Technical logs: deleted after 7 days.
  • Usage and cost records: kept to keep an eye on costs. They hold no messages and no email addresses.

Who else handles it

  • Amazon Web Services (Ireland) runs the app and its database.
  • An AI model provider (Anthropic, or Amazon Bedrock) receives your messages to write the answers.
  • Google, if you choose "Continue with Google": it tells the app your email address and name.
  • Gmail sends the app's emails.
  • LangSmith may receive traces of conversations for fixing problems, with card numbers, email addresses and other personal details masked first.

Please don't

Type real card numbers, passwords or bank details. Everything here is pretend.

Your choices

Press "Start over" to replace your demo bank and clear your conversations, or simply leave: everything is deleted after 24 hours. For questions, or to have something deleted sooner, email __CONTACT__.
